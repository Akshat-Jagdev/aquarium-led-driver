Before soldering: ① with a multimeter, confirm each 5-hole column is one node and check whether your rails are continuous or split in halves (many solderable BBs split them — if split, bridge with wire). ② Columns are counted from the left, 1–63; rows a–e top bank (a nearest top rail), f–j bottom bank. ③ Tin everything first; AL8860 legs = 30AWG wire. ④ The J8–J13 board joints and colour-series chains from the PCB design do NOT apply here — one perfboard replaces all four boards, so each channel drives one LED string directly.

Bring-up sequence & test points

StepActionExpect
0LM2596 module ALONE on bench: feed 24V, set pot12.00 V at OUT, before any wiring in
1Wire power section only (cols 1–8, rails, C1/C2). No ICs soldered yet+24V rail = 24V, GND continuity OK, fuse holds
2Solder CH1 (U1 white) only. White LED string ≈17.6V at (4,d)–(5,c). Power via switchV across RSET (1,c)–(4,c) ≈ 100 mV → I=250 mA; SW node square wave; SS3100 warm not hot
3If current off-sim, swap RSET (try 0.47/0.50Ω)settles the open RSET decision → final PCB BOM
4Add CH2–CH4 whites one at a timeeach ≈100 mV across its RSET; total 24V current ≈0.95 A
5Wire U10 + C12V; verify +12V rail unloaded12.0 V
6Add CH5/CH6/CH7 (12V channels) one at a timeR:122 mA · B:303 mA · DR:122 mA (100 mV/RSET)
7Logic section + ESP32 PWMCTRL 3V3 → full current; 0V → off
⚠ U8 IRM-02-5 flag: BOM lists Mean Well IRM-02-5 (24V→5V) but that module's input range is 70–305 VAC mains — it will NOT start from +24V DC. For the prototype power the ESP32 from USB. Re-check before the PCB order (swap to a 24→5V DC-DC, e.g. LM2596-5.0 mini module).
