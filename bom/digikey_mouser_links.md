## Sourcing Strategy

| Part Type | Primary | Backup | Reason |
|-----------|---------|--------|--------|
| ICs, passives, RSETs | LCSC | DigiKey/Mouser | LCSC is 30-50% cheaper |
| Connectors, switches | Robu / Quartz | DigiKey | Indian stock = fast delivery |
| Power supplies | Sunrom / Evelta | DigiKey | Mean Well distributor pricing |
| Aluminium extrusion | Local hardware | IndiaMART | Shipping aluminium is expensive |
| MCPCB | JLCPCB | Indian fab (PCBPower) | JLC for low qty, Indian for bulk |
| TIM (thermal pad) | Robu | Mouser | Indian stock |
| Acrylic diffuser | Local plastic | IndiaMART | Custom cut is cheaper local |
| Mounting brackets | Amazon IN / local | IndiaMART | Off-the-shelf products |
| Test equipment | Amazon IN | Robu | Indian stock |
