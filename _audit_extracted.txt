3. Other ﬁndings
CTRL pin buffering (2N7002) is likely unnecessary. Datasheet: CTRL pin is rated for
direct 3.3 V/5 V logic drive, open-drain or push-pull. Drop the MOSFET stage unless
there's a speciﬁc isolation reason. If keeping it, verify the exact 2N7002 variant's
Vgs(th) — generic parts can spec up to 2.5–3 V, marginal at 3.3 V GPIO drive.
AL8860QMP-13 vs AL8860MP-13. You ordered the Q (automotive-grade) variant.
Conﬁrm you actually need AEC-Q100 — if not, the standard AL8860MP-13 is very
likely cheaper at volume. Verify pin/electrical parity against the AL8860Q datasheet
before substituting.
White channel duty (73%) is close to the 75% recommended ceiling. The 5.85 V Vf
ﬁgure is described as "typical at the speciﬁed test condition" — conﬁrm that test
current in the Luxeon 5050 HE Plus datasheet; if it's quoted at a higher current than
your 250 mA target, real Vf (and real duty) will be lower, which is a safety margin in
your favor, but should be conﬁrmed rather than assumed.
Input capacitor: datasheet's own recommendation is 4.7–10 µ F X7R, which is what
you have — no change needed. (X7R DC-bias derating is real physics but already
accounted for in this range per Diodes' own guidance.)
4. Open items before PCB layout
1. Source a 24 V→12 V pre-regulator for Rail B (small buck module or second IC — not
yet speced).
2. Source a 100 µ H shielded inductor (Rs/DCR comparable to the Würth 68 µ H part) for
the 660 nm channel only.
3. Conﬁrm actual Vf of white LEDs at 250 mA from the Luxeon 5050 HE Plus datasheet
(not the "typical test condition" ﬁgure).
4. Conﬁrm AL8860 vs AL8860Q pin/electrical parity if switching part numbers.
5. Bench-verify t_ON/f_SW on red, blue, and 660 nm channels on Rail B once pre-
regulator is in place — these are calculated values, not yet measured.
